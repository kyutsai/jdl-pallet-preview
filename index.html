Why we do this

Good for the forest. Good for your budget.

Wood is precious, even where there's plenty of it. Every new pallet is built from fresh-cut lumber, and many are thrown out after a single trip. That wastes wood and money. So we fix pallets that still have years in them and sell them for less than new.

Fewer trees cut

A sound pallet goes straight back into service. No new lumber, nothing thrown away.

Repair before replace

One cracked board shouldn't scrap a whole pallet. We replace the board and keep the rest working.

Lower cost than new

A recycled pallet does the same job as a new one for a lower price. Ask us for a quote and compare.
